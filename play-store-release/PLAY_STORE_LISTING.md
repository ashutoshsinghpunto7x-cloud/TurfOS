## Full Description (4000 char max)

```
Playbox is the all-in-one platform for booking and managing sports turf/ground time slots — built for players, turf owners, and staff alike.

⚽ FOR PLAYERS
• Browse real-time slot availability for your favorite turf
• Book instantly or request a slot for owner approval
• Pay securely online via Razorpay (cards, UPI, netbanking) or offline at the venue
• Get instant on-device notifications for booking confirmations and slot reminders
• Track your upcoming and past bookings in one place

🏟️ FOR TURF OWNERS & STAFF
• Manage your turf's daily bookings from a live calendar
• Approve or decline online booking requests
• Run a built-in Point-of-Sale (POS) for on-site sales and rentals
• Track inventory, customer credit ledgers, and generate reports
• Create and manage coupons and promotions
• Schedule internal matches/leagues and track live scores
• Mark and monitor staff attendance
• Role-based access for Owners, Admins, and Staff — everyone sees exactly what they need

🔒 SECURE BY DESIGN
• Payments are processed by Razorpay — your card and bank details never touch our servers
• Your data is protected with encrypted storage and secure authentication

Whether you're booking your weekly football match or running a multi-turf sports facility, Playbox brings scheduling, payments, and operations together in one simple app.

Download Playbox today and take the hassle out of turf booking and management.
```
