1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect: full name, email address, and password (stored securely and hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth — we never see or store your plaintext password).

1.2 Booking & Contact Information

When you book a turf/ground slot, we collect your phone number and booking details (date, time slot, sport, turf selected, price).

1.3 Payment Information

Online payments are processed by Razorpay, a third-party PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Card numbers, UPI IDs, and other sensitive payment credentials are entered directly into Razorpay's secure checkout and are never transmitted to, or stored by, Playbox's servers or app. We store only the payment outcome (order ID, payment ID, status). For manual/offline payment, you may optionally upload a payment screenshot as proof.

1.4 Photos

The App lets you optionally pick an image from your device's photo gallery (e.g., a payment screenshot). The App does not access your camera directly and does not access your photo library except when you actively choose to pick an image.

1.5 Notifications

The App requests permission to send local, on-device notifications (e.g., booking confirmations). These are not sent via a third-party push service, and we do not collect a device push token.

1.6 Staff Attendance Data (Staff/Owner Accounts Only)

For Staff role users, the App records the date and time attendance is marked. No location data is collected.

1.7 Automatically Collected Information

Our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase) may automatically log technical information such as IP address and request timestamps for security and abuse-prevention purposes.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise or approximate device location (GPS)
  • Direct camera access
  • Advertising SDK data
  • Third-party analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and confirm turf/ground bookings
  • Facilitate payments via Razorpay
  • Send booking-related notifications
  • Allow turf owners/staff to manage bookings, customers, and attendance
  • Maintain security and integrity of the App

4. Data Storage & Security

Account, booking, and profile data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL with row-level security). Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Session tokens are stored on-device using secure, OS-level encrypted storage where available. Payment card/UPI data is handled entirely by Razorpay.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with Supabase (backend provider), Razorpay (payment processor), and the relevant turf's owner/staff (to fulfil your booking). We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and booking data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
